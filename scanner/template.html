How the scanner reads the books

1. Trend Template (all 8 must pass)

  • Price above the 150 and 200-day averages
  • 150-day above the 200-day
  • 200-day rising for at least a month (ideally 4-5)
  • 50-day above the 150 and 200-day
  • Price above the 50-day
  • At least 25% above the 52-week low
  • Within 25% of the 52-week high
  • RS rating 70+ (ranked against every liquid stock in the same market)

2. The base and the VCP

The base starts at the highest high of the last 65 weeks. The scanner measures each pullback (a "T") from the deepest one rightward. A valid VCP has 2-6 Ts that get smaller, a base no deeper than 50% (ideally under 35%), a final contraction of 10% or less, and volume drying up near the pivot. The footprint reads like the book: 8W 22/2 3T is an 8-week base that corrected 22% at its deepest and 2% at its tightest, with 3 contractions.

3. Status

  • Breaking out: cleared the pivot in the last 1-3 sessions and is no more than 5% above it. Check that volume is at least 40% above average.
  • Buy-ready: a valid setup with price within 5% under the pivot. Set an alert at the pivot.
  • Setting up: base forming, not tight yet.
  • Trend only: passes the template but no buyable base.
  • Extended: more than 5% past the pivot or 20% above the 50-day. Don't chase; wait for the next base.

4. Other setups

  • Power play: up 100%+ in under 8 weeks on heavy volume, then a 3-6 week flag that corrects no more than 20-25%. The only setup Minervini buys without fundamentals on the table.
  • Cheat / low cheat (3-C): a tight 5-10% pause one-third to two-thirds of the way up a 15-50% cup, on light volume.

5. Score

Out of 100: RS rating (25), base quality (25), fundamentals (20), closeness to the 52-week high (10), months of rising 200-day (5), up/down volume (5) and an early base count (5). Bases 1-2 after a correction are the best; bases 4+ are late stage.

ASX versus US

  • Each market is ranked and judged on its own: RS ratings compare ASX stocks only with ASX stocks, and the market condition uses the S&P/ASX 200 or the S&P 500.
  • ASX stocks need A$1M a day of turnover to be scanned; US stocks need US$10M, because institutions are what move leaders.
  • The prompts change with the market: ASX versions use 4D/4E, 4C/5B, Appendix 3Y and ASIC short data; US versions use 10-Q, 8-K, Form 4 and 13F.
  • US positions carry overnight gap risk for you in Brisbane and A$/US$ currency risk.

6. Risk rules built into the trade plan

  • Risk 1.25-2.5% of your account per trade
  • Stop no wider than 10%; aim for 5-6% average losses
  • Best names 20-25% of the account, never more than 50% in one stock
  • Move the stop to breakeven once the gain is 2-3 times your risk
